How a bill moves through Congress. Each stage produces a new official text.

  1. ih/is — Introduced (House/Senate). First filed.
  2. rfh/rfs — Referred to committee.
  3. rh/rs — Reported back by committee (often with amendments).
  4. pcs/pch — Placed on Calendar for floor vote.
  5. eh/es — Engrossed. Passed originating chamber.
  6. rdh/rds — Received by the other chamber.
  7. eah/eas — Engrossed Amendment. Other chamber passed amended version.
  8. ath/ats — Agreed to. Both chambers settled on same text.
  9. enr — Enrolled. Final text sent to President.
  10. pl — Public Law. Signed.
  11. pp — Public Print. Official post-enactment printing.
