Session-marker heatmap mocks

Two ways to surface "Congress in session vs recess" on a per-legislator activity heatmap, without overpowering the activity signal.

Synthetic data: 52 weeks of activity (light/medium/heavy days), with House & Senate session schedules roughly mirroring real 119/1 calendar (in-session Tue–Thu most weeks, recess Aug + late Dec, plus state-work-period blocks).

Baseline (current)

No session indication. Recess days look identical to in-session quiet days.

Option A — muted bg on recess empty tiles

Empty squares get a darker cream when both chambers were in recess that day. Active days unchanged. Subtlest possible — easy to miss.

Option B — stripe under grid (House + Senate)

Two thin rows under the heatmap: top = House, bottom = Senate. Colored bar where in session, transparent where recess. Heatmap untouched — context lives below.
